Our story • Our faith • Our purpose

Compassion with a purpose.
Service rooted in faith.

From a burden to serve vulnerable people to practical programmes in education, skills and community development, our story is one of faith becoming action.

How it began

A calling to serve, a commitment to restore dignity.

Kireta Care Foundation was established in 2016 by Kireta Richard. Its beginnings were shaped by prayer, evangelism and a desire to see Christian compassion make a tangible difference in everyday life. Through church and community encounters, the founder became increasingly aware of the challenges facing orphaned children, vulnerable families and people living with limited opportunities.

Families were facing food insecurity, illness, unemployment and barriers to education. The response was not to look away, but to serve. Early efforts included providing food and other basic necessities, facilitating free medical services, supporting children’s schooling and introducing vocational training in areas such as tailoring and building-related work.

As the work grew, the Foundation explored ways to sustain its activities through local enterprise and personal commitment. Bricklaying and brick-making, poultry farming and other practical initiatives helped create resources and demonstrate that community development can be built from local skills, effort and cooperation.

Over time, this journey led to the establishment of Kireta Care Primary School and a tailoring facility, alongside wider community initiatives. The Foundation now works from a more structured base, with a continuing commitment to serve with integrity, stewardship and compassion.

Our vision

Communities where every person can live with dignity and hope.

We envision children who can learn, families who can provide, young people who can contribute, and communities strengthened by faith, opportunity and mutual care.

Our mission

Serving people and strengthening possibility.

Our mission is to improve the wellbeing of vulnerable individuals and communities through Christian service, education, health support, vocational skills, agriculture, child and family support, and sustainable community development.

What guides us

Our values in practice

Faith

We seek to reflect the love of Christ through humility, prayer and practical service.

Compassion

We listen, recognise people’s dignity and respond to real needs without discrimination.

Integrity

We act honestly and responsibly, using resources with care and transparency.

Stewardship

We nurture local resources, skills and relationships for the benefit of future generations.

Empowerment

We aim to strengthen people’s capacity rather than create long-term dependence.

Community

We believe lasting progress is strongest when people participate and work together.
